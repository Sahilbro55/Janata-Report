🚨 Emergency Helplines

Important Contact Numbers

Keep these numbers handy. For life-threatening emergencies, call immediately — do not wait to file an online report.

🚨 If you or someone around you is in immediate danger — call emergency services right now. These numbers are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

🔴 Life Emergency Numbers

👮

Nepal Police

Crime, accident, public safety
100

Call for crimes in progress, road accidents, public disturbances, missing persons, or any situation where you need immediate law enforcement presence.

📞 Call 100
🚒

Fire Brigade

Fire, rescue, hazardous situations
101

Call immediately for any fire — home, vehicle, forest, or industrial. Also covers structural collapses, gas leaks, and rescue operations.

📞 Call 101
🚑

Ambulance Service

Medical emergencies, injuries
102

For medical emergencies requiring urgent transport — severe injuries, heart attacks, strokes, difficult childbirth, or any life-threatening condition.

📞 Call 102

⚠ Before You Call — Quick Tips

  • Stay calm and speak clearly when connected to an operator
  • State your exact location first — street name, ward number, landmark
  • Describe what is happening in a few short sentences
  • Do not hang up until the operator tells you it is okay to do so
  • If calling about someone else, stay with them if it is safe
  • For electricity faults, do not touch wires — call NEA immediately
  • For fire, leave the building first, then call 101 from outside
  • Keep these numbers saved on your phone right now, not just this page
📋

Not an Emergency? File a Report Instead.

These helplines are for urgent situations. For ongoing civic problems — a pothole that has been there for a month, a streetlight that has been dead for weeks, a garbage bin that never gets collected — please use Janata Report. That way the complaint gets logged, tracked, and followed up properly.
