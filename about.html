🏛 What is Janata Report?

Janata Report is a civic issue reporting platform designed to connect citizens with local authorities in a simple and efficient way. It gives every Nepali the ability to report problems — electricity failures, road damage, water issues, garbage pileups, broken streetlights — directly to the people responsible for fixing them.

For too long, civic complaints have been stuck in long queues at ward offices, lost in phone calls, or simply ignored because there was no organised way to track them. Janata Report changes that. Every complaint gets a unique ID, a public status, and a place on the dashboard that anyone can check.

This platform was created to improve communication between citizens and service providers, ensuring faster response times and greater transparency in how public services are managed and delivered.

Janata Report is developed by Misfits and is based in Ghorahi, Dang, Nepal. We started serving communities in 2082 B.S. with a simple belief — a reported problem is already halfway to being solved.

🎯 Our Mission

Make Civic Reporting Accessible

To make civic issue reporting simple, fast, and accessible for everyone — whether you are in a city ward or a remote municipality. No complicated paperwork. No running between offices. Just a form, a submit button, and a system that tracks what happens next.

🔭 Our Vision

A More Responsive Public Service

A Nepal where public services are transparent, responsive, and accountable — powered by citizen participation. We envision every ward office equipped with the tools and the data to act quickly, and every citizen empowered enough to demand it.

🌟 Who Benefits and How

👤 For Citizens

  • Report issues from anywhere, any time — no office visit needed
  • Get a unique Report ID to track your complaint's progress
  • See all reported issues in your area on the public dashboard
  • Share your GPS location for faster on-site response
  • Upload a photo to give authorities a clear picture of the problem
  • Know when your issue moves from Pending to Resolved

🏛 For Authorities

  • Receive organised, categorised complaints in one place
  • GPS coordinates help teams locate issues without confusion
  • Update status in real time — citizens see changes immediately
  • Filter reports by category, ward, or urgency for better planning
  • Build trust by showing citizens that reports are being acted on
  • Reduce time wasted on phone calls and walk-in complaints
