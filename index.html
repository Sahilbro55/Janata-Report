Issue Categories

What Can You Report?

From electricity outages to flooded roads — if it's a community problem, this is where you flag it.

⚡

Electricity

Frequent power cuts, faulty transformers, dangling wires, or billing problems with your local NEA office.

💧

Water Supply

No water supply, leaking pipelines, dirty tap water, or pressure too low to be of any use.

🛣️

Road Damage

Potholes, broken footpaths, clogged drains, or debris blocking the road after heavy rain.

🗑️

Garbage

Garbage trucks skipping your street, illegal dumping near homes, or overflowing community bins.

💡

Streetlights

Lights out for days, dark alleys that feel unsafe at night, or lights on in broad daylight.

📋

Other Issues

Noise pollution, public park damage, illegal encroachments, or anything else affecting your neighbourhood.

The Process

Simple. Fast. Transparent.

Filing a report takes about 2 minutes. Here is what happens from the moment you submit.

1

Login and Report

Log in as a citizen, fill a short form — category, location, description, and a photo if you have one.

2

Gets a Unique ID

Every complaint gets tagged with a unique Report ID the moment it is submitted, so nothing gets lost.

3

Authority Reviews

The relevant ward office or department sees the complaint and marks it as In Progress when work begins.

4

Marked Resolved

Once fixed, the report closes as Resolved. The full timeline stays visible on the public dashboard.
